# Claims this archive does not treat as fact

**Label: analysis.** An October 5, 2026 fact-check of an earlier research memo listed claims it could not verify. They are recorded here so a later page does not promote them into the case files. A line in this list is not a finding that the claim is false. It is a finding that this archive does not have the source.

The fact-check compared twelve dated Wayback copies of `uap-data.csv` and went back to the House letter, NASA image pages, and FOIA postings it could open. Where that check corrected the memo, the correction is in the case pages and the manifests, with a source. The items below are the ones that stayed unverified.

| # | Claim | Why it stays unverified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| U1 | A 1962 University Daily Kansan quotation in which Schirra says the "fireflies" look like ice crystals. | The fact-check could not retrieve the newspaper page. The substance (ice or frozen water) is in Schirra's 1998 oral history, which is a different source and was not re-opened for this archive. |
| U2 | The Robertson Panel watched a film of seagulls, criticized the Navy photo method, and the Condon Report or Blue Book later said the Tremonton objects were probably gulls. | CIA-UAP-002's catalog description does not mention Tremonton or gulls. The later Air Force line in this catalog is "seabirds," in DOW-UAP-D102, D103, and D105. The panel and Condon sentences were not checked in the underlying PDFs. |
| U3 | The Navy photo lab's Tremonton study appears in an official release for the first time in PURSUE. | Not checked against the National Archives Blue Book microfilm. Leave it as an assessment, or leave it out. |
| U4 | An Air Force photo lab considered birds and balloons and found them doubtful, according to a NICAP page. | That page was not fetched. |
| U5 | The FAA formed a task force on the 2019–2020 Colorado and Nebraska drone reports and never determined what was flying. | Not confirmed in the news items the fact-check read. This archive's [drone page](../cases/colorado-nebraska-drones-2019-2020.md) does not use it. |
| U6 | Harald Malmgren said NASA and the White House kept a Schirra "tag-along" out of public statements. | Advocate posts describe a tag-along claim, an Atlas claim, and a Bluegill claim. That specific "kept out of public statements" wording was not found. The interview was not reviewed here. See [Schirra](../cases/schirra-mercury-atlas-8.md). |
| U7 | Rep. Tim Burchett called the releases "a drop in the bucket." | Found by the fact-check only in a USA Herald article. No video or primary post was located. |
| U8 | USA Today and The Northwestern covered the Apollo 17 image on May 8, 2026. | The Northwestern URL was behind a paywall. The path date is consistent with May 8. The article was not read here. |
| U9 | AAWSAP modifications P00002 through P00005 had never been public before Release 06. | They were not found on The Black Vault. Other FOIA postings are not ruled out. Do not call them new. See [AAWSAP](../cases/aawsap.md). |
| U10 | Details of a March 6, 2026 letter led by Rep. Eric Burlison: co-signers, and the claim that the letter itself is not public. | The letter is not in this archive. See [the House letter note](../sources/house-oversight/README.md). |
| U11 | pursueufotracker.com's account that the Release 01 CSV was restructured on May 11, from 161 rows to 158, by consolidating duplicate PDFs. | One advocate source. No May 8–19 CSV is stored here. The 162-versus-158 gap is real as a press count versus the May 20 CSV. The consolidation story is not. |
| U12 | A separate wave of drone reports hit New Jersey in late 2024. | Not checked. It is not a PURSUE file. |
| U13 | FBI file 62-HQ-83894 was long publicly available through The Vault. | vault.fbi.gov was not checked. The catalog description itself says the file is partially posted on the FBI's Vault, with more redactions and some pages missing, and that the PURSUE copy adds newly declassified pages. That catalog sentence is official. "Long publicly available" is the part that was not re-checked. |
