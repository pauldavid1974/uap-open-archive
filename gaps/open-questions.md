# Open questions

**Label: analysis.** These are questions the catalog raises and does not answer. They are not a to-do list of beliefs.

## Promised and not posted

- The Apollo 17 three-dot note says a full NASA and Department of War analysis "will be released when completed." It is not a row in the September 29, 2026 catalog, and the October 4, 2026 archived homepage still showed Release 06 as the latest tranche. See [Apollo 17](../cases/apollo-17-three-dots.md).
- Each AAWSAP DIRD description says the paper is one of 38. The spreadsheet contains 37 DIRD titles (D117–D153). The missing paper is not named in those descriptions. This archive did not re-fetch the 2022 DIA FOIA release that secondary sources say withheld a classified laser paper. See [AAWSAP](../cases/aawsap.md).

## The spreadsheet disagrees with itself

Tracked in [catalog quality](../analysis/catalog-quality.md). The ones that change a story, not just a spelling:

- Puerto Rico: description says November 19, 1964; the date cell says 2/1/65.
- Several mission-report titles name a country and a year that the date and location cells do not match.
- FBI-UAP-D014 is two different files.
- DVIDS id 1006111 is attached both to a 2026 Army video and to FBI Photo A001. The DVIDS page is the Army video.
- DVIDS id 1007720 is attached to both PR057a and PR057b. The DVIDS page is PR057a.

## Numbering holes

DOW-UAP-D, DOW-UAP-PR, and FBI-UAP-D skip numbers. The Department has not said whether a missing number was withheld, reported later as a resolved case, or never used. The PURSUE page says resolved cases are reported separately, as statute requires. It does not name a website for that reporting. The list is generated in [catalog quality](../analysis/catalog-quality.md).

## Agencies and sensors

No NSA, NRO, FAA, DHS, or Space Force name appears in the spreadsheet. No radar plot or sensor-parameter file is described for the modern videos. See [absent agencies](../analysis/absent-agencies.md).

## Famous cases with no named file

A title-and-description search found no Nimitz, Gimbal, GoFast, FLIR1, Roswell, Rendlesham, Phoenix Lights, Kecksburg, Socorro, Shag Harbour, Project Twinkle, Condon Committee, or Malmstrom/Minot file. "Tic Tac" appears only on two 2024 Coast Guard titles. Details and the exact search strings are in [keyword search](keyword-search.md). A miss does not prove the topic is absent from inside a PDF nobody opened.

## Claims left unverified on purpose

Thirteen secondary claims, including a gull conclusion by the Robertson Panel, an FAA task force, and a story that Release 01's CSV was rebuilt on May 11, are listed in [unverified claims](unverified-claims.md). They are not used as facts anywhere else in this archive.

## What "unresolved" hides

The PURSUE page says only unresolved cases are posted here, and resolved ones are reported elsewhere. A reader who sees only this site sees the remainder. The size of the remainder is not in the catalog.
