## Who is not in the column

A search of the whole spreadsheet for these names returned no hits: National Security Agency, National Reconnaissance Office, Federal Aviation Administration, Department of Homeland Security, Space Force.

The Coast Guard is not an agency value. The words "USCG" do appear in two video titles from 2024 ([DOW-UAP-PR065](../records/dow-uap-pr065.md) and [PR066](../records/dow-uap-pr066.md)).

## Why that matters

**Label: analysis.** The modern videos are mostly infrared clips without coordinates, altitude, or radar. The agencies that routinely hold radar, air-traffic, and satellite data are not originators of any row in this catalog. AARO's own descriptions often say there is no technical data or that a clip is a phone filming a screen. The absence and the missing metadata are the same problem seen from two sides. The Department of War has not, in the pages this archive read, explained the absence as a policy.

Resolved cases are also absent by design. The PURSUE page says those are reported separately. See [the release timeline](../timelines/releases.md).
