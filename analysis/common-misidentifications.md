# Ordinary explanations that are already in the files

**Label: analysis.** This page only lists explanations the official catalog itself states. It does not import a skeptic's theory and call it a finding. Where outside readers disagree, the case file says so and labels it.

## Patterns the descriptions repeat

**Label: analysis**, drawn from wording that shows up across many video rows, not from a private theory.

- "Area of contrast" is the catalog's phrase for a spot on an infrared screen. It avoids the word object.
- "Secondary capture" means someone filmed a screen with a phone. The Gulf of Oman clips say this. The original recorder was not working.
- "Digitally altered before upload" appears on the batch of videos tied to the March 6, 2026 congressional request, including the Syria clip, and on some older clips the catalog says were transferred by the UAP Task Force.
- Phone autofocus "may distort" shape and motion. That sentence is on the Colorado police videos.
- Several descriptions say the paragraph is not an analytical conclusion. Believe the caution. It is doing work.

## What this page refuses

A clip can still be interesting after an ordinary explanation is offered, and an official explanation can be wrong. This page does not grade the whole catalog as "all balloons" or "all real." Rows that carry no explanation stay unexplained. Their case files say so.
