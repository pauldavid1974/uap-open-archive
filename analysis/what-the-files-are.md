# Two piles of files, plus a third

**Label: analysis.** The split below is a reading of incident dates and titles in the September 29, 2026 catalog. It is not a category the Department of War prints.

## Recent military and police sensor files

Most rows whose dates fall in the 2010s and 2020s are short infrared videos, mission reports, range-fouler debriefs, or FBI interviews. The catalog's own descriptions, over and over, omit coordinates, altitude, and sensor settings. Many are flagged redacted. Some are phones filming a screen. Some were digitally altered before they reached the archive. AARO often says it is not offering a conclusion.

This is the pile that looks like "new footage" and is the hardest to check.

## Older paper

A large minority of rows are files from the 1940s through the 1970s: Air Force projects, FBI case files, CIA memos, State Department cables, NASA mission transcripts. Many are not flagged redacted. Many had already been public in some form. The catalog sometimes says so, for example on [CIA-UAP-015](../records/cia-uap-015.md), which says that apart from a handwritten note the Blue Book special report had been on the CIA's public website.

This pile is easier to read and easier to over-claim. A document being old and official does not make its contents true. It makes the wording checkable.

## Contract papers

Release 06 adds the AAWSAP contract file and 37 technical reviews. They are not sightings. The catalog says they should not be read as validation of warp drives or antigravity. See [AAWSAP](../cases/aawsap.md).

## Counts, roughly

**Label: analysis.** A precise era split needs a judgment on every vague date ("Late 2025," "2020," "N/A"). This archive does not pretend that judgment is exact. The catalog has 450 rows: 270 documents (264 cells typed `PDF` plus 6 typed `PDF` with a trailing space), 134 videos, 30 images, and 16 audio files. Release-by-release tables are on each [manifest](../sources/pursue-01/manifest.md).

The point of the split is practical. If a memory is "a 1960s pilot and a radar ship," start with [Puerto Rico](../cases/puerto-rico-radar-1964.md), not with a 2024 infrared clip. If a memory is "orbs at a test range, a helicopter, a senior official," start with [late 2025](../cases/western-us-test-range-orbs-2025.md), not with the 2023 Western United States Event.
