## Days between releases

**Label: analysis.** May 8 to May 22 is 14 days. Then 21, 28, 28, and 42 days. The gaps got longer. The PURSUE page says tranches would be posted every few weeks. It does not promise a fixed schedule.

## What officials said at the time

**Label: official.**

- February 19, 2026. The PURSUE page quotes a Truth Social post by President Trump directing the Secretary of War and other agencies to identify and release government files on alien and extraterrestrial life, UAP, and UFOs. The page is the source used here, not a direct capture of the social-media post.
- May 8. The Department says many files had been reviewed for security but not yet analyzed for resolution. Secretary Hegseth, Director Gabbard, Director Patel, and Administrator Isaacman are quoted. [Extract](../sources/press/2026-05-08-release-01.md).
- May 22. Sean Parnell says WAR.GOV/UFO had received over 1 billion hits since May 8.
- May 26. The catalog entry for [ODNI-UAP-D001](../records/odni-uap-d001.md) records a typo correction ("map-of-the-earth" to "nap-of-the-earth"). The date is the catalog's note. This archive does not hold a May 26 CSV. The corrected wording is already present in the September 18 snapshot.
- June 12. Parnell says the hit count was over 1.7 billion since May 8.
- July 10, August 7, and September 18. Parnell announces the next tranche. Those three statements do not give a hit count or a file count.
- September 18, 11:39 UTC. Release 06 has 71 catalog rows: four Colorado videos, no transcripts.
- September 18, 15:56 UTC. Transcript LLE-UAP-D001 is in. The release has 72 rows.
- September 29. Transcripts D002, D003, and D004 are in. The release has 75 rows. [Details](../analysis/catalog-quality.md).

## Where a resolved case would go

**Label: official.** The PURSUE page says the files archived there are unresolved, and that the Department will keep reporting resolved cases separately, as statute requires. This repository therefore cannot show the solved majority, if there is one. It only has what was posted here.
