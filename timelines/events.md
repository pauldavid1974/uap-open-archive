# A short chronology of events

This is not every date in the catalog. It is the sequence a reader needs in order to tell the well-known episodes apart. The full incident-date column is in [data/records.csv](../data/records.csv). Dates below are the dates the catalog states, unless a label says otherwise.

**Label: official** unless marked.

| When | What | Where to read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1947–1949 | Project Sign. An Air Materiel Command progress report covers 100 sightings. | [Project Sign](../entities/project-sign.md) |
| 1948–1950 | Hundreds of "green orb," disc, and fireball reports near Sandia Base, New Mexico. The catalog says some of this work fed Project Grudge. | [DOW-UAP-D017](../records/dow-uap-d017.md) |
| March 26, 1952 | Capt. Edward Ruppelt, in Boston, says about 20 percent of roughly 800 cases were still unexplained. | [Edward Ruppelt](../entities/edward-ruppelt.md) |
| July 2, 1952 | Delbert Newhouse films objects near Tremonton, Utah. Later official writing disagrees about birds, balloons, and unknowns. | [Tremonton](../cases/tremonton-1952.md) |
| 1952–1953 | CIA scientific panel (often called the Robertson Panel) says flying saucers are not a direct threat and recommends debunking. | [Panel](../entities/robertson-panel.md) |
| October 3, 1962 | Wally Schirra describes particles coming off his capsule, and a flash of light at sunset. | [Schirra](../cases/schirra-mercury-atlas-8.md) |
| May 1962–May 1963 | Carpenter and Cooper describe snowflakes and "John's fireflies." NASA's catalog entry on Cooper says the fireflies were frozen condensation. | [Particles](../cases/astronaut-particles.md) |
| November 19, 1964 | A Navy pilot and the destroyer USS Gyatt, near Puerto Rico. The catalog's date *cell* on one memo says February 1, 1965. The description says November 19, 1964. | [Puerto Rico](../cases/puerto-rico-radar-1964.md) |
| December 1972 | Apollo 17 photograph with three dots. Full analysis promised, not in this catalog. | [Apollo 17](../cases/apollo-17-three-dots.md) |
| July 18, 2008 | AAWSAP statement of objectives. Award to BAASS follows on September 22, 2008. | [AAWSAP](../cases/aawsap.md) |
| September 1, 2015 | Unidentified object reported over the Pantex plant near Amarillo, Texas. | [Pantex](../entities/pantex-plant.md) |
| December 2019–January 2020 | Colorado and Nebraska drone reports. **Not in the PURSUE catalog.** Listed so they are not confused with 2023. | [Drones](../cases/colorado-nebraska-drones-2019-2020.md) |
| September 8, 2021 | AC-130J crew reports cold orbs over the Gulf of Oman. Released video is a phone filming the screen. | [Gulf of Oman](../cases/gulf-of-oman-orbs-2021.md) |
| 2021 | Infrared clip later titled "Syrian UAP instant acceleration." The catalog says the sensor stopped tracking. | [Syria clip](../cases/syria-instant-acceleration.md) |
| 2023, two days | Western United States Event. Seven federal employees. AARO: no technical data, still unresolved as of June 2026. | [Western US Event](../cases/western-us-event-2023.md) |
| October 2023 | Colorado officer films a silent multicolored light. Town and time of night withheld. | [Colorado police](../cases/colorado-police-2023.md) |
| Late 2025 | Orbs at a western U.S. test installation, including a senior intelligence official in a helicopter. | [2025 orbs](../cases/western-us-test-range-orbs-2025.md) |
| February 19, 2026 | Presidential direction quoted on the PURSUE page. | [Releases](releases.md) |
| May 8–September 18, 2026 | Six PURSUE releases. | [Releases](releases.md) |

Famous cases that are not in this table (Nimitz 2004, Gimbal, Roswell, Rendlesham, and others) were searched for in the catalog and not found as named files. See [keyword search](../gaps/keyword-search.md).
