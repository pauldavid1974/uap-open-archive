## What the letter itself says

The letter is dated March 31, 2026. It is addressed to Secretary Pete Hegseth. It is signed by Anna Paulina Luna, Chairwoman of the Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets. It copies Ranking Member Jasmine Crockett. It asks that the listed videos be delivered no later than April 14, 2026. The deadline is in the letter. It does not depend on a later news account.

The letter lists **46 numbered items**. Item 11 has sub-videos a through c. Items 26 and 36 each have sub-videos a and b. Counted that way, the letter covers **50 video files**. Items 13 and 14 have the same title.

Item 1 is "4 UAP formation – Iran, 8/26/22." Item 2 is "Syrian UAP instant acceleration, 2021." Item 46 is "Hi-Res: Hackney 4X observes UAPs at 1715Z, 9/23/19."

## How this differs from the March 6 request

**Label: official** for the catalog sentence. **Label: analysis** for the comparison.

Many Release 02 video descriptions say that on March 6, 2026, eight members of the House asked for 51 potentially UAP-related records. That is a different request from this March 31 letter. The March 6 letter is not in this folder. Who led it, and who signed it, is not in the catalog sentence, and this archive does not have the letter. Those details stay [unverified](../../gaps/unverified-claims.md).

Release 02's video run from PR050 through PR099 is 51 records, counting PR057a and PR057b. PR050's title matches item 1 of this letter. PR051's title matches item 2. An October 5, 2026 fact-check lined the run up against the letter and found the same order, with the extra record over the letter's 50 files likely to be PR060 ("obj 2"). This archive confirmed the first two titles and the 46-item / 50-file count from the PDF. It did not re-check every later title against the letter. PR099's title is a "Hi-Res" observation at 1715Z, which is the shape of item 46, and the catalog title says 25SEP19 where the letter says 9/23/19. Both dates are printed in those two places. Neither was edited.
