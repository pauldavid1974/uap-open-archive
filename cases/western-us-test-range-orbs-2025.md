# Late 2025 orbs at a western U.S. test installation

**Status: unexplained.** The catalog publishes a senior intelligence official's account and a set of stills. It does not identify the objects.

## In plain language

In late 2025, government people at a sensitive testing installation in the western United States reported glowing orbs. One account is from a senior U.S. intelligence official who was a passenger in a helicopter. Another is an FBI interview with a senior official. Two sets of pictures were released with the interview. The A-series descriptions say the stills come from a U.S. government system. The B-series descriptions say they come from a U.S. military system. The catalog's own words for the picture are a small dark spot in a grainy frame with a crosshair. They do not show a resolved craft.

Do not mix this up with the 2023 Western United States Event. The catalog dates are different.

## What the official catalog says

**Label: official.** [USPER Statement about UAP Sighting](../records/usper-statement-about-uap-sighting.md), Release 01, an FBI interview: the official and others searched an area where orbs had been seen. After a helicopter search they found a "super-hot" orb hovering over the ground. The orb is reported to have traveled 20 miles at a speed too fast for the helicopter. A "swarm" of lights moved in all directions. Four or five further orbs flared up and down over about thirty minutes. The same description says the photos are tied to encounters on a sensitive government testing installation in the western United States in 2025, seen by multiple government personnel and sensors, sometimes at the same time. The official saw them with the naked eye, with two pilots using night-vision goggles. The photos under the helicopter were taken through night-vision devices.

**Label: official.** [ODNI-UAP-D001](../records/odni-uap-d001.md), Release 02, is a first-person account by a senior intelligence official who was still serving in May 2026. From the helicopter he describes glowing orbs, including a low, fast object that appeared to split in two and accelerate away in two directions, and higher orbs he thought were close to the helicopter. The catalog notes a May 26, 2026 correction: "map-of-the-earth" was changed to "nap-of-the-earth." That date is the catalog's note. The corrected wording is already in the September 18 snapshot stored here. This archive does not hold a May 26 or May 29 CSV. An October 5 fact-check of other Wayback copies reported that the new wording first appears in a May 29 snapshot, not in a May 23 one.

**Label: official.** [FBI Photo A001](../records/fbi-photo-a001.md) through A008: each description says the still was derived from a U.S. government system. Date and location were not provided on A001's row. The imagery was altered with redactions before it was sent to AARO. The description says a small, dark, slightly irregular object sits just below and right of a crosshair, and that this description is not an analytical conclusion. A001–A008 are catalog type image.

**Label: official.** [FBI Photo B001](../records/fbi-photo-b001.md) through B024: each description says the still was derived from a U.S. military system in 2025. They are catalog type PDF. This archive did not open them, so it does not know whether each PDF is a single still. Do not describe the A series as a military system. The catalog uses "government system" for those eight.

## A catalog error to know about

**Label: analysis.** The spreadsheet puts DVIDS id 1006111 on both FBI Photo A001 and on [DOW-UAP-PR049](../records/dow-uap-pr049.md), a 2026 Army video. The DVIDS page for that id, retrieved October 6, 2026, is the Army video, not the FBI still. The photo's war.gov image link is a separate URL. See [catalog quality](../analysis/catalog-quality.md).

## Strongest and weakest points

**Label: analysis.**

- Strongest: more than one official account, including a named class of witness (a serving senior intelligence official), plus stills the government says came from its own system during the same episode.
- Weakest: the published still is, by the catalog's description, a small dark mark. Locations on the photo rows are often blank or "N/A." No coordinates or sensor settings are in the descriptions. A test range is exactly where secret aircraft fly. The catalog does not rule that in or out.

## Outside comment

**Label: analysis.** This archive did not re-read press or skeptic write-ups of this episode. Do not treat a summary from somewhere else as if it were in these files.
