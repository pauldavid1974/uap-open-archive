# Colorado and Nebraska drone reports, December 2019–January 2020

**Status: disputed.** This wave is not a PURSUE case. It is here because people mix it up with the October 2023 Colorado police videos that *are* in the catalog.

## What this archive can say

**Label: analysis.** A search of all 450 PURSUE titles and descriptions (see [keyword search](../gaps/keyword-search.md) for the method, and `data/records.json` for the text) finds the October 2023 local-police clips. It does not find a file whose title or description is the December 2019–January 2020 reports of drone formations over northeast Colorado and western Nebraska.

The 2023 police case is one officer and one light. The 2019–2020 reports, as they are discussed in news coverage, were a regional wave. They are different events. See [Colorado police videos](colorado-police-2023.md).

## What this archive will not repeat

**Label: press.** News organizations and the Colorado Department of Public Safety have written about the 2019–2020 reports. On October 6, 2026 a request to `publicsafety.colorado.gov` from this project returned HTTP 403, so the state's own update was not re-read. Counts that circulate in secondary write-ups (how many reports, how many were hobby drones, how many were stars) are not copied here, because they were not re-checked against the state page.

A starting URL, not a verified quotation: <https://publicsafety.colorado.gov/press-release/updates-on-investigations-into-suspicious-drone-activity-in-ne-colorado>.

## Why the date matters

**Label: analysis.** Calling this wave "late 2022" or "2023" puts it on top of a different Colorado file. The police videos in PURSUE are dated October 2023 in the catalog. The regional drone reports that made national news were the winter of 2019–2020. That correction is why this page exists, even with no PURSUE record attached.
