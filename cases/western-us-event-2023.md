# Western United States Event, 2023

**Status: unexplained.** The All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office says that, as of June 2026, the case remained unresolved. This page did not open the underlying PDFs.

## In plain language

Over two days in 2023, seven federal employees separately described unusual things in the sky or near the ground in the western United States, near what AARO calls a sensitive national security site. AARO has called the report one of the most compelling in its holdings. The same official summary says there is no technical data with it. No radar file, no sensor settings, and no coordinates are in the catalog description.

This is not the late-2025 test-range orb case, and it is not the separate "FBI September 2023 Sighting" papers. Those are different catalog rows.

## What the official catalog says

**Label: official.** From the summary row [Western US Event](../records/western-us-event.md), Release 01:

The document summarizes statements by seven U.S. persons employed by the federal government. They reported four kinds of experience: "orbs launching other orbs" at a distance, a large stationary glowing orb at close estimated range, pursuit of a large phenomenon near the ground, and a large seemingly transparent phenomenon "akin to a translucent kite." The summary says there is no technical data directly associated with the report, and that context (shared features with other AARO reports, the reporters' credibility, and the nature of the events) makes it "among the most compelling within AARO's current holdings."

**Label: official.** [DOW-UAP-D077](../records/dow-uap-d077.md), Release 03, says AARO's analysis was still open: "As of June 2026, the case remains unresolved."

Later rows add a notional map (D078), five narrative statements (D079–D083), FBI digital renderings, and two digital recreations. Renderings and recreations are drawings or reconstructions. The titles say so. They are not described as sensor photographs.

## Strongest and weakest points

**Label: analysis.**

- Strongest: several federal employees, over two days, and AARO's own sentence calling the report among its most compelling, while also saying it was still unresolved in June 2026.
- Weakest: the government's own summary says there is no technical data. Without a place, a time of day, or a sensor file, an outside person cannot test ordinary explanations. This archive did not open the PDFs, so it cannot add detail the catalog left out.
