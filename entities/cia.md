# Central Intelligence Agency

**Label: official.** The CIA is the agency on 23 catalog rows. They include the 1952–1953 Scientific Advisory Panel report ([CIA-UAP-002](../records/cia-uap-002.md)), Project Blue Book Special Report No. 14, Cold War sighting memos, and the Puerto Rico memoranda ([CIA-UAP-D022](../records/cia-uap-d022.md) and [D023](../records/cia-uap-d023.md)).

**Label: analysis.** The CIA uses more than one id style in the spreadsheet (`CIA-UAP-002` and `CIA-UAP-D022`, for example). That is a numbering fact, not two agencies.
