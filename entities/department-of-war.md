# Department of War

**Label: official.** The Department of War is the agency name on the PURSUE website and on 257 of the 450 catalog rows in the September 29, 2026 spreadsheet. It is the department that hosts [war.gov/UFO](https://www.war.gov/UFO/) (archived October 4, 2026: <https://web.archive.org/web/20261004165250/https://www.war.gov/UFO/>).

The page says the Department, with support from the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, is overseeing a government-wide effort to find, review, declassify, and release unresolved UAP records. It says the posted material is unresolved, "meaning the government is unable to make a definitive determination," and that resolved cases are reported separately.

**Label: analysis.** "Department of War" is the name the department is using in 2026. Older files inside the releases were created by the War Department, the Air Force, the Navy, and other predecessors. The catalog's agency cell does not always name the historical office that wrote the paper. The description sometimes does.

This page is not a history of the department. It is a pointer to how the name is used in this archive.
