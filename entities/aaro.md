# All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office

**Label: official.** AARO is the Department of War office named in the May 8, 2026 press release as part of PURSUE, and named throughout the catalog descriptions as the office that receives reports and writes many of the video notes.

The archived PURSUE homepage says resolved cases are reported separately from this site. The May 8 release says many of the materials had been reviewed for security but not yet analyzed for resolution.

**Label: analysis.** This archive could not open aaro.mil on October 6, 2026 (HTTP 403). Pages here quote AARO only where the PURSUE catalog or a Department of War press page says something. They do not summarize AARO's annual reports or its 2024 historical report, because those PDFs were not retrieved.

Catalog descriptions often say "area of contrast" instead of "object," and they often say a description is not an analytical conclusion. That wording is AARO's, as printed in the spreadsheet.
