# Edward J. Ruppelt

**Label: official.** [DOW-UAP-D154](../records/dow-uap-d154.md) is a transcript of a March 26, 1952 presentation in which Captain Edward J. Ruppelt describes the Air Force's reorganized UFO investigation. The catalog says he reported that of about 800 historical cases, about 20 percent remained unexplained; that unresolved reports came from military personnel, commercial pilots, and other technical observers; that reports clustered around Los Alamos, Albuquerque, and Oak Ridge; and that some radar indications suggested unconfirmed speeds of 1,000 to 3,500 miles per hour. He is quoted, via the catalog, as saying the Air Force had no conclusive evidence of what the phenomena were. The audio is [DOW-UAP-PR160](../records/dow-uap-pr160.md).

This page is not a biography.
