# Pete Hegseth

**Label: official.** Quoted on the PURSUE homepage and in the May 8, 2026 press release as Secretary of War. The sentence posted with the first release: "The Department of War is in lockstep with President Trump to bring unprecedented transparency regarding our government's understanding of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena. These files, hidden behind classifications, have long fueled justified speculation — and it's time the American people see it for themselves."

Source: [press extract](../sources/press/2026-05-08-release-01.md) and the [archived PURSUE page](https://web.archive.org/web/20261004165250/https://www.war.gov/UFO/).

This page is not a biography. It records the quotation attached to these files.
