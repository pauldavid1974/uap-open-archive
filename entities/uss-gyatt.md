# USS Gyatt

**Label: official.** Named in [CIA-UAP-D022](../records/cia-uap-d022.md) and [CIA-UAP-D023](../records/cia-uap-d023.md) as a guided-missile destroyer whose AN/SPS-49 radar tracked a contact near Puerto Rico at about 3,800 knots on the incident the memos date to November 19, 1964. D023 says the track lasted 21 minutes. See [the case](../cases/puerto-rico-radar-1964.md).

This page is not a ship history.
