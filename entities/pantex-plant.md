# Pantex Plant

**Label: official.** [DOE-UAP-D005](../records/doe-uap-d005.md) is described as imagery and a report of a September 1, 2015 incident: an unidentified object in the airspace above the Pantex Plant near Amarillo, Texas. The catalog says the plant is the primary U.S. facility for assembly, disassembly, maintenance, and life-extension of nuclear weapons. It says pages 5 and 6 were posted earlier, more heavily redacted, as [DOE-UAP-D001](../records/doe-uap-d001.md) on May 22, 2026.

The PDFs were not opened for this archive. There is no separate case file yet, because the catalog description does not go past that summary.
