# PURSUE

**Label: official.** Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters. The name is expanded on the [archived war.gov/UFO page](https://web.archive.org/web/20261004165250/https://www.war.gov/UFO/) (October 4, 2026).

That page says the program responds to President Trump's February 19, 2026 direction to identify and release government files on alien and extraterrestrial life, UAP, and UFOs. It says the Department of War, with ODNI support, is reviewing records across agencies, including paper files, and posting unresolved cases in tranches "every few weeks."

Six tranches had been posted by the October 4, 2026 snapshot: May 8, May 22, June 12, July 10, August 7, and September 18, 2026. The release timeline is [here](../timelines/releases.md).
