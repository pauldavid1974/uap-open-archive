# Project Sign

**Label: official.** [DOW-UAP-D097](../records/dow-uap-d097.md) is described as an initial 1948 report from Air Materiel Command on Project Sign, "a 1948-1949 U.S. Air Force program" to investigate unidentified flying objects, covering 100 sightings from 1947–1948.

[DOW-UAP-D100](../records/dow-uap-d100.md) is described as memoranda on the same 1948 program. The catalog says some reports were characterized as consistent with real objects rather than fabrications, and that reported performance exceeded known U.S. aircraft if the reports held up. It also says, in the same family of files, that the Air Force wanted more evidence. Read the record rather than a one-line moral. The PDF was not opened here.
