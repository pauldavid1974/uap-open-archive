# Delbert C. Newhouse

**Label: official.** The catalog does not use one title for him, and this page does not collapse them.

- [DOW-UAP-D102](../records/dow-uap-d102.md) calls him "a U.S. Navy Warrant Officer specializing in photography." It says Blue Book assessed him as honest and reliable, with more than 1,000 hours of aerial photography, and that he reported 12 to 14 reflective objects near Tremonton, Utah, on July 2, 1952.
- [DOW-UAP-PR159](../records/dow-uap-pr159.md) calls him "U.S. Navy Chief Warrant Officer Delbert C. Newhouse."
- [DOW-UAP-D104](../records/dow-uap-d104.md) is described as his final Navy personnel record. It says he left naval service as a Chief Warrant Officer 4, and that his enlisted specialty had been Photographer's Mate.

He is not described in these rows as a "chief photographer."

This page is not a biography. It only records how the catalog names him. See [Tremonton](../cases/tremonton-1952.md).
