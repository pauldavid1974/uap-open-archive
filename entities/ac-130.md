# AC-130J

**Label: official.** [DOW-UAP-D101](../records/dow-uap-d101.md) attributes the September 8, 2021 Gulf of Oman report to the aircrew of a U.S. Special Operations Forces AC-130 gunship. [DOW-UAP-PR117](../records/dow-uap-pr117.md) specifies an AC-130J and says the released video is a cellphone filming the infrared display, not the aircraft's own recorder. The report says that recorder was not working. See [the case](../cases/gulf-of-oman-orbs-2021.md).
